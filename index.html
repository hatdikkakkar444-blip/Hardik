Some things I don't say out loud enough

Somya, you walk into a room and it quietly rearranges itself around you. Nobody asks it to. It just does, and I'm lucky enough to be standing close when it happens.

A princess, I think, isn't the one with the crown. She's the one who makes ordinary days feel like they were planned in advance: the long phone calls, the inside jokes nobody else would understand, the way you make a bad day softer without even trying.

You are patient with my chaos, kind when you don't have to be, and brave in the ways that never make the headlines. I notice all of it. I'm learning something new about you every week, and I hope that never stops.

So today is yours. No schedules, no worries. Just a reminder that you are adored, exactly as you are, by someone who is very proud to be yours.

Always yours,Hardik
